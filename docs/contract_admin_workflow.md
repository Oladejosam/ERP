# Contract Admin Workflow

Contract Admin follows a practical building-contract lifecycle:

1. **Opportunity and procurement strategy**: identify the scope, client, estimated value, route to market, and bid deadline.
2. **Tender and evaluation**: preserve issue, clarification, submission, evaluation, approval, and recommendation milestones.
3. **Award and pre-start**: record the winning bidder, awarded value, award approval, contract execution, insurance/bonds, programme, and commencement readiness.
4. **Contract administration**: keep dated records of instructions, variations, extensions of time, payment applications/certificates, claims, risks, and decisions.
5. **Completion and closeout**: record practical completion, defects, final account, handover, warranties, retention release, and lessons learned.

The module stores the contract register and dated process notes. Formal files can remain in the existing Project document register once the tender becomes a project.

## Research basis

- UK Cabinet Office, [The Construction Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-construction-playbook), published 8 December 2020 and updated 11 July 2023. It describes expectations for assessing, procuring, and delivering public works.
- The lifecycle above is a software-oriented synthesis of common construction procurement and administration practice. Contract terms, approval thresholds, procurement law, and retention/claims rules should be configured for the company and jurisdiction before production use.
